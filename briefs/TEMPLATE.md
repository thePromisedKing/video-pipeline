# Video script template

Copy this to `briefs/<slug>.md` (or just paste it into `/video`), fill in what you know, delete the rest.
Claude turns it into `videos/<slug>.json`, finds or records the visuals, and renders.
Anything left blank, Claude drafts and asks you to approve.
